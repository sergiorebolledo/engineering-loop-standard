# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to make AI-assisted engineering more disciplined, not
less humane. The same standard applies here: be direct about technical
disagreements, and respectful about the people having them.

Examples of behavior that helps this project:

- Giving specific, actionable feedback on issues and PRs ("this breaks
  `doctor` when `memory.required_files` is empty" beats "this is wrong").
- Assuming good faith — most disagreements here are about tradeoffs
  (portability vs. simplicity, strictness vs. flexibility), not mistakes.
- Welcoming first-time contributors and questions about the spec, even
  when the answer is "that's covered in docs/SPECIFICATION.md §3."

Examples of behavior that isn't acceptable:

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissive language directed at a person
  rather than their argument or code.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, PRs, discussions, or private contact
  made because of this project.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to
representing the project in public spaces (conference talks, social media)
under this project's name.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a GitHub issue if it's safe to do
so publicly, or by contacting the maintainer directly via their GitHub
profile for anything sensitive. Reports will be reviewed and may result in
a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from project spaces, at the
maintainer's discretion, proportional to the severity of the behavior.
